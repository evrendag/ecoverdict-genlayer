# GenLayer Portal submission

## Project name

ECOVERDICT

## One-sentence summary

ECOVERDICT audits public environmental claims against live evidence and issues challengeable, versioned onchain Green Claim Passports through GenLayer consensus.

## Description (under 1,000 characters)

ECOVERDICT is a complete GenLayer application for testing public environmental claims such as “100% renewable,” “carbon neutral” or “recyclable.” A claim owner freezes the exact wording, scope, review standard and validity window, then seals 2–6 public HTTPS sources. Independent GenLayer validators fetch those live sources and assess period, boundary, geography, baseline, methodology, offsets and contradictions. The contract finalizes SUBSTANTIATED, QUALIFIED, MISLEADING or INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE. Verdict, evidence quality, material qualification and contradiction count must match exactly; scores share a band and stay within 10 points. Deterministic checks reject inconsistent outputs and broken evidence fails closed. A different wallet can add counter-evidence and trigger a new audit; the previous passport is preserved as SUPERSEDED. The frontend manages the complete claim-to-passport lifecycle, and other apps can reuse `is_substantiated`.
