# Differentiation

ECOVERDICT is not a generic fact checker, ESG score, document summarizer or
“ask an LLM whether this sounds green” demo.

Its primitive is a versioned, challengeable evidence passport for the exact
wording and scope of a public environmental claim. The contract itself retrieves
the sealed sources, enforces an environmental-claim-specific verdict taxonomy,
rejects cross-field inconsistencies, preserves lineage and exposes a reusable
onchain predicate.

It is also separate from PROOFCOURT. PROOFCOURT audits whether an assigned AI
agent completed a work order. ECOVERDICT has no worker, delivery, job or escrow;
it audits public claims whose evidence can change over time and provides a
third-party counter-evidence and re-audit path.
